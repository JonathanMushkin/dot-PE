# End-to-end GPU runner: GW240525_201644 on one L40S

This engineering phase checks that `dot_pe_gpu.run` runs a production configuration end to end on
one GPU, writes a rundir that the o4b tools read, and reproduces a production run.

The results were made with dot-PE branch `gpu-v2` (commits f9479b6 to 397f72c), on one NVIDIA L40S
with 8 cores. The data are o4b GW240525_201644 run_2: 3 banks of 2^20 templates, n_ext = 13312,
n_phi = 100, `size_limit` 1e7, made with dot-PE 2403731 in 21022 s on 8 cores. Supersedes: none.

## Question and answer

Does the GPU runner reproduce a production run, and how long does it take? Given run_2's
incoherent selection and extrinsic samples, it reproduces run_2's summary:
1. ln_evidence to 6e-6;
2. n_effective_i and n_effective_e to 1e-4 (relative);
3. the LVK-prior summary from o4b's `reweight_rundir` to 3e-6.

The like-for-like rerun took 780 s, and a fresh run with nothing recycled took 722 s. Both
times are wall time on one L40S, against 21022 s in production.

## Gates (from `design.md`)

1. Test 1 completes, and its rundir has the CPU rundir's files except `mp_coherent_setup_*`.
   Passed.
2. Test 1 against run_2. Passed:

   |---|---|---|
   | ln_evidence | −2.826964 | −2.826958 |
   | n_effective_i | 1534.83 | 1534.66 |
   | n_effective_e | 262.216 | 262.204 |
   | bestfit_lnlike_max | 35.28719 | 35.28684 |
   | lnl_marginalized_max | 29.22481 | 29.22480 |
   | n_distance_marginalizations | 36965353576 | 36965355809 |

   Per bank, |Δ ln_evidence| ≤ 2.5e-5.
3. o4b compatibility. Passed. `lvk_reweight.reweight_rundir` (o4a-pe env, o4b code imported
   read-only) ran on the test-1 rundir. Its LVK-prior ln_evidence is −4.052861, against run_2's
   −4.052864, and its n_effective_i is 232.04, against 232.03.
4. Test 2 completes, and its incoherent selection equals run_2's up to templates at the threshold.
   Passed. The selections are 158130, 219064 and 217315 templates, against 158131, 219065 and
   217314: one template per bank, at the threshold 24.66, as in P3.

## Setup

1. Test 1 recycles run_2's `intrinsic_samples.npz` per bank and its `extrinsic_samples.feather`,
   in bestfit mode, so the selected waveforms are read from disk in stage 5.
2. Test 2 recycles nothing and uses `row_selection="phase_marginalized_priority"`. Stage 2
   streams all three banks and keeps the selected waveforms on the device (14.4 GB), so stage 5
   reads no file.
3. Both are shared jobs. Their timings are therefore indicative; P5 times exclusive jobs.

## Stage times (seconds)

| Stage | run_2 (8 cores, CPU) | GPU test 1 | GPU test 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 setup | 20.9 | 14.2 | 17.3 |
| 2 incoherent | 1962.5 | 0.5 (loaded) | 474.3 |
| 4 extrinsic | 170.0 | 1.3 (loaded) | 130.5 |
| 5 coherent | 18793.3 | 743.8 | 79.8 |
| 6 postprocess | 43.7 | 19.8 | 19.8 |
| total | 21022 | 780 | 722 |

In test 2:
1. Stage 2 spends 128 to 139 s per bank waiting for disk reads, at 0.37 GB/s with 32 reader
   threads. P3's exclusive timing job read a cold bank at 0.87 GB/s.
2. In stage 5, the device blocks take 18 to 22 s per bank (1162 i-blocks in all), and the
   per-bank setup and writes take 6 to 7 s.

## Limitations

1. Disk read throughput varies between jobs by a factor of about 2.4 (0.37 to 0.87 GB/s), and it
   sets the stage-2 time.
2. run_2 was made with cProfile enabled, which inflates its CPU times.
3. The test-2 priority-mode results are not compared here; their correctness was tested per block
   in P2.

## Reproduction

`python research/lsf_submit.py --name p4_test1 --env dot-pe-gpu --cores 8 --mem-mb 64000
--walltime-min 30 --gpu NVIDIAL40S --gmem 24 --end-to-end -- -m dot_pe_gpu.run --event x
--bank-folder x --n-ext 1 --n-phi 1 --n-t 1 --kwargs-json research/runner/test1_kwargs.json`
runs test 1; `test2_kwargs.json` runs test 2. `research/runner/o4b_compat.py RUNDIR` runs the
o4b check, in the `o4a-pe` env.
